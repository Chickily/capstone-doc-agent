Acme Corp Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Japan Standard Time.
Remote employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
Equipment requests, such as monitors or keyboards, must be submitted through the IT service desk
and are approved within five business days.
Expense Policy
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 5,000 yen per day.
All expenses over 20,000 yen require written approval from a department head.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.
